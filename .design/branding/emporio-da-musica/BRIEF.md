## Business
- **Problem:** Beginner and intermediate musicians in the Midwest of Brazil struggle to choose the right instrument. Big marketplaces dump specs they can't read, and store clerks push the most expensive option.
- **Solution:** Honest, knowledgeable guidance over WhatsApp from people (and now an agent) who actually play: real stock, real prices, clear payment options (PIX discount, interest-free installments) and fair return policies.
- **Business model:** Retail margin on instruments; installment plans; free local delivery above R$ 500.
- **Defensibility:** Trust and expertise in a regional market, a curated instruments-only catalog, and a service relationship the big marketplaces can't replicate.

## Personas

### Primary: Luísa
- **Role:** Self-taught guitarist, early-career professional
- **Age range:** 22–28
- **Day-in-the-life:** Works during the day in Campo Grande and practices at night with YouTube lessons. Lives on WhatsApp, and buys online when she trusts the seller.
- **Frustration:** Online listings bury her in specs she doesn't understand, and in-store clerks push the priciest instrument.
- **Aspiration:** Someone who understands music, talks like a person and honestly tells her which instrument fits her. Up to about R$ 1,500, possibly in 12 installments, available any time on WhatsApp.
- **Discovery:** Friends, YouTube teachers, Instagram, and asking the store directly on WhatsApp.
- **Trust signals:**
  - **Trust:** straight answers, real prices with the original price shown next to any discount, being told "you don't need to spend more".
  - **Distrust:** pushy upsell, fake urgency, robotic replies, jargon without explanation.

## Brand Essence

### Emotional Compass
- **brand_heartbeat:** "Conversar no balcão de madeira com quem toca há anos: sem pressa, sem empurrão, só a verdade sobre o que vai soar bem nas suas mãos." (Talking across a wooden counter with someone who has played for years: no rush, no push, just the truth about what will sound good in your hands.)

### Promise
- **Core promise:** Every conversation should feel like getting advice from a musician friend who happens to work at the store.
- **Functional promise:** Accurate prices, stock, delivery and return rules, every time, with no guesswork.
- **Emotional promise:** Confident and respected, never pressured or talked down to.

### Point of View
- **Category disagreement:** Instrument retail treats beginners as either a spec sheet or an upsell target.
- **Underestimated truth:** The right instrument is often the cheaper one, and saying so builds more loyalty than any discount.
- **Manifesto line:** The best sale is the instrument you'll actually love playing.

### Personality
- **Personality:** warm, honest, knowledgeable
- **Personality reference:** Like Fender Play's patient teaching meets Nubank's plain-spoken clarity, with the soul of an independent record shop.
- **Not us:** robotic, snobbish
- **Never be:** A customer-service robot (stock phrases, SAC formality, lists and walls of text). A posh store that intimidates (unexplained jargon, condescension).
- **Tone:** Informal but professional, "como um amigo que entende de música" (store manual, section 7.1).

## Competitive Landscape
- **Direct competitors:** National marketplaces (Mercado Livre, Amazon), large instrument chains and their e-commerce, and other local music stores.
- **What sets you apart?** Honest, regional, instruments-only expertise delivered conversationally on WhatsApp.
- **Brands admired:** Fender Play (didactic, encouraging), Nubank (plain language, human tone), independent record shops (warmth, curation).

## Visual Direction
- **Mood / aesthetic:** A wooden shop in warm light: guitar-wood tones (honey, walnut), cream, and a terracotta accent. Humanist, rounded typography.
- **Reference links:** Guitar spruce and cedar tops, late-afternoon light in a small music shop, handwritten price tags.
- **Texture / atmosphere:** Subtle paper grain and wood-grain textures; photography of hands and instruments in golden-hour light.
- **Anti-patterns:** Corporate stock photos, cold blues and greys, marketplace product grids, aggressive sale banners.

## Inspiration
- **Styles liked:** Warm, crafted, editorial-but-friendly.
- **Styles to avoid:** SaaS-generic, luxury-minimal that intimidates, discount-marketplace loud.
- **Existing assets:** None. Store data comes from the case files only (address, hours, policies).

## Constraints
- **Timeline:** Design phase must fit the case deadline (Mon 2026-11-09).
- **Budget:** N/A (case project).
- **Must-haves:**
  - WCAG 2.2 AA;
  - mobile-first (customers arrive from WhatsApp);
  - UI copy in Brazilian Portuguese.
- **Non-negotiables:** No third-party copyrighted assets (fonts must be open-licensed; no copyrighted photos).

## Goals
- **Business goal:** Answer recurring customer questions accurately and warmly, freeing the staff.
- **Brand goal:** A recognizable, trustworthy voice shared by the agent and the interface.
- **Success metrics:** Eval voice score ≥ 4.5/5 with zero AI tells; WCAG 2.2 AA with no critical issues; the evaluator perceives a human, honest store.
