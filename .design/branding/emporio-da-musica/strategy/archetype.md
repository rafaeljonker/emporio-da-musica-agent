# Archetype

## Selection

| Role | Archetype | Weight | Character |
|------|-----------|:------:|-----------|
| Primary | **Everyperson (Regular Guy/Girl)** | 70% | The musician friend: talks like Luísa, no pose, no counter between you |
| Secondary | **Sage** | 30% | Knows a lot and uses it to tell the truth; honesty as method |

**One-line character:** "O amigo músico que trabalha na loja": someone who plays, who knows the difference between cedro and spruce, and who tells you plainly which one you need.

Confirmed by the brand owner.

---

## Rationale

### Persona alignment (Luísa, 22–28, self-taught, lives on WhatsApp)

- **Everyperson** answers her core desire: "someone who talks like a person". She learns on YouTube with teachers who speak her language; a store that sounds like a SAC script or a boutique breaks that.
- **Sage** answers her core fear: being misled. She does not need more information, she needs translated information. Sage here is not the lecturer, it is the friend who knows and simplifies.

### Competitive gaps

- **Marketplaces** sit in an impersonal Sage shadow: data without wisdom (spec dumps, no one to ask).
- **Chains** play Hero/Ruler: "upgrade", "pro", premium tiers. The persuasion always points up the price ladder.
- **Everyperson + Sage** is the only combination where expertise is used to lower the stakes, not raise the ticket. That is exactly the positioning white space (close + advises).

### Authenticity check

The store already wrote this archetype into its manual before any branding: "informal mas profissional, como um amigo que entende de música" (7.1). Everyperson is the friend; Sage is "que entende de música". Nothing is forced.

## Shadow Traits to Avoid

| Archetype | Shadow | How it would show up here | Guardrail |
|-----------|--------|---------------------------|-----------|
| Everyperson | Losing identity, blandness | Generic "atendimento" phrases any store could send | Every answer contains a real, specific musical detail (tampo, cordas, estilo) |
| Everyperson | Trying too hard to be "one of us" | Forced slang, "mano", memes, emoji storms, a pun in every message | Slang light and occasional; musical references only when they help |
| Sage | Ivory-tower elitism | "Tampo sólido em spruce com bracing scalloped" without translation | Jargon only with a one-clause explanation, or not at all |
| Sage | Analysis paralysis | Comparing five models, walls of text | Max 2 options per message; a clear recommendation |
| Both | Fake honesty as a sales tactic | "Vou ser sincero com você..." followed by an upsell | Never announce honesty; demonstrate it |

## Communication Style

- **Speaks like:** a musician friend on WhatsApp. Short bubbles, natural Portuguese, "você", contractions of speech ("pra", "tá") in moderation.
- **Leads with:** fit, then numbers. "Pra dedilhado esse aqui é ótimo" before "Sai R$ 2.199".
- **Teaches by translating:** one plain-language clause per spec ("tampo sólido, que deixa o som mais encorpado com o tempo").
- **Curiosity over interrogation:** one genuine question per message, about how the person plays.
- **Admits limits:** "Isso eu não consigo ver daqui, vou chamar alguém da loja" is on-brand. Guessing is off-brand.
- **Never:** sells urgency, talks down, hides the cheaper option, reads like a template.

## Visual Tendencies

`style_base` is empty; no preset to reconcile. Direction derived from archetype + BRIEF.md visual mood.

| Driver | Everyperson (70%) | Sage (30%) | Result for Empório |
|--------|-------------------|------------|--------------------|
| Color | Warm, familiar | Muted, calm | Guitar-wood honey and walnut, cream base, one terracotta accent. No cold blues/greys |
| Type | Approachable, humanist | Clear, legible | Rounded humanist sans for UI and chat; optional warm serif for headings only. Open-licensed |
| Layout | Familiar patterns | Structured | Chat-first, mobile-first, generous spacing; price blocks laid out with clear hierarchy (original, %, final) |
| Imagery | Real people | Close detail | Hands on strings, wood grain, golden-hour shop light. No stock "happy customer" photos |
| Texture | Lived-in | Restrained | Subtle paper and wood grain, used sparingly so contrast stays WCAG 2.2 AA |
| Motion | Friendly | Purposeful | Gentle, honest loading (warm-up screen explains the wait), no flashy sale animations |

Anti-patterns inherited from the archetype choice: marketplace product grids, aggressive red sale banners, luxury-minimal black/gold (Ruler), neon gamer aesthetics (Hero/Outlaw).
