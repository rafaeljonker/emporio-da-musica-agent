# Messaging

> Phase: strategy | Brand: emporio-da-musica | Generated: 2026-11-05

---

## Core Message

**EN:** We tell you the truth about instruments, even when the truth is that the cheaper one is enough.

**pt-BR:** A gente fala a verdade sobre instrumento, até quando a verdade é "esse mais barato resolve".

Traces to: Luísa's frustration ("clerks push the priciest instrument") and her top trust signal ("being told you don't need to spend more").

---

## Supporting Messages

### 1. Advice from someone who plays
**pt-BR:** "Antes de falar de preço, a gente quer saber como você toca."
- Proof: every recommendation is tied to style, level and comfort (value *Falar a língua de quem toca*).
- Proof: specs translated into sound ("tampo sólido, som mais encorpado"), never dumped.
- Proof: instruments-only catalog since 2008, 300+ models curated by people who play.
- Answers: "online listings bury me in specs I don't understand".

### 2. The whole price on the table
**pt-BR:** "Preço de verdade: o original, o desconto e o final, sem letra miúda."
- Proof: promo always shown as original + % + final (store manual 6.2).
- Proof: PIX 5% off; up to 12x sem juros (minimum installment rules applied by the system); free delivery in Campo Grande metro above R$ 500, R$ 35 below.
- Proof: no fake urgency; stock claims only from the system; expired promos stated as expired.
- Answers: distrust of "fake urgency" and hidden conditions.

### 3. Safe to say yes
**pt-BR:** "Comprou online e não era isso? Você tem 7 dias pra devolver, e o frete de volta é com a gente."
- Proof: 7-day regret right on online purchases, return shipping paid by the store, refund in up to 10 business days.
- Proof: 30-day exchange for manufacturing defects; 90-day legal warranty.
- Proof: honest handoff to staff when the agent does not know; complaints answered within 24 business hours.
- Answers: the risk Luísa feels buying online "only when I trust the seller".

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

**EN:** Empório da Música is an instruments-only store in Campo Grande, open since 2008. Most beginners get stuck between marketplaces that bury them in specs and store clerks who push the most expensive model. We do the opposite: we ask how you play, explain what actually matters for your sound, and show the real price, PIX and installments included. And if the cheaper instrument is the right one, we say so. Now you can get that same advice on WhatsApp, any time.

**pt-BR:** A Empório da Música é uma loja só de instrumentos, em Campo Grande desde 2008. Quem tá começando costuma ficar entre o marketplace cheio de ficha técnica e o vendedor que empurra o mais caro. A gente faz diferente: pergunta como você toca, explica o que importa pro seu som e mostra o preço de verdade, com PIX e parcelamento. E se o mais barato for o certo pra você, a gente fala. Agora dá pra ter essa conversa no WhatsApp, a qualquer hora.

## Tagline Directions

| # | Tagline (pt-BR) | Rationale | Swap test |
|---|-----------------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | **"A verdade de quem toca."** | Brand essence in four words. Sage (verdade) + Everyperson (quem toca). Works on the login screen, signature and storefront. **Recommended primary.** | A marketplace has no "quem toca"; a chain cannot own "verdade" while upselling. Passes |
| 2 | **"Às vezes, o mais barato resolve."** | The bold play, stated. Memorable because no store says it. Best as a campaign / social line, not a permanent signature (it would read as a discount brand if overused). | No upsell-driven competitor would print it. Passes |
| 3 | **"Pergunta pra quem toca."** | An invitation that names the channel behavior (ask on WhatsApp). Good CTA-tagline for chat entry points. | Slightly weaker: another local store could use it. Pair with the Empório name |

Existing manual slogan "Sua música começa aqui." fails the swap test (any music store or school could sign it). Keep it only as legacy internal copy; do not use it in the new voice system.

## Narrative Arc

1. **Setup:** You want to start playing (or play better) and you open a marketplace or walk into a big store.
2. **Tension:** Fifty spec sheets you can't read, or a clerk steering you to the R$ 4 mil model.
3. **Resolution:** A musician friend at Empório asks how you play, translates what matters and shows the real price.
4. **Transformation:** You leave with the instrument you'll actually love playing, often for less than you feared, and you tell a friend.
