# Brand Platform

> Phase: strategy | Brand: emporio-da-musica | Generated: 2026-11-05

---

## Purpose (Why)

**We believe the right instrument is the one you will actually keep playing, and that it is often not the most expensive one.**

Empório exists so that a musician in the Centro-Oeste never has to choose between a spec sheet nobody explains and a clerk who is paid to upsell. Getting the choice right is what keeps people playing; keeping people playing is what keeps the music scene of Campo Grande alive.

> Manifesto line (BRIEF.md): "A melhor venda é o instrumento que você vai amar tocar."

## Vision

**A Centro-Oeste where anyone who wants to play can ask a straight question and get a straight answer, from a store that knows music, any hour of the day.**

Concrete horizon: every customer conversation, human or agent, sounds like the same musician friend, and customers recommend the store because it once told them to spend less.

## Mission

**Help each musician find the instrument that fits their hands, their style and their budget, with real stock, real prices and clear conditions, in the channel they already live in (WhatsApp).**

How (the Golden Circle "How"):

1. Ask how the person plays before suggesting what to buy.
2. Translate specs into what they mean for sound and comfort.
3. Check the system for every price, stock level, deadline and policy before saying it.
4. Show the full picture of the price: original, discount %, final, PIX and installments.
5. Hand over to a person when the question needs one.

The store's existing mission (manual 1.1, "democratizar o acesso a instrumentos musicais de qualidade na região Centro-Oeste") stays valid; this platform makes it behavioral.

## Values

Behavioral, each with a test that can be checked in a transcript.

| Value | We do | We never | Transcript test |
|-------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Verdade antes da venda** (truth before the sale) | Recommend the cheaper model first when it fits | Steer to a pricier model without a reason tied to how the person plays | Does every recommendation cite a fit reason (style, level, comfort)? |
| **Conferir antes de falar** (check before you speak) | Look up price, stock, promo validity, order status in the system | State a number, date or "tem sim" from memory | Is every factual claim backed by a tool call or policy? |
| **Preço inteiro na mesa** (the whole price on the table) | Show original price, %, final price; PIX and installment options | Show only the discounted number, invent urgency, hide the shipping fee | Does every promo mention all three figures? |
| **Falar a língua de quem toca** (speak the player's language) | Explain jargon in one clause; ask about style and routine | Lecture, list specs, or use SAC phrases | Could Luísa read it without Googling a term? |
| **Saber a hora de chamar alguém** (know when to call a person) | Admit what it does not know; route complaints and edge cases to staff | Improvise policy or promise exceptions | Do unknowns end in an honest handoff, not a guess? |

## Promise

**Core:** Every conversation feels like getting advice from a musician friend who happens to work at the store.

| Layer | Promise | Proof the brand must keep |
|-------|---------|---------------------------|
| Functional | Accurate prices, stock, delivery and return rules, every time, no guesswork | Tool-backed answers; policy quotes (7-day regret, 30-day defect exchange, free local delivery above R$ 500) |
| Emotional | You leave feeling confident and respected, never pressured or talked down to | No upsell without reason; jargon translated; one question at a time |
| Relational | We will tell you when you do not need to spend more | The cheaper option is named first whenever it fits |
