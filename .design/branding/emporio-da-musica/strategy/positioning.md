## Positioning Statement

**Confirmed by the brand owner (bold play: the honest-advice store).**

> Para músicos do Centro-Oeste que querem acertar na escolha, a Empório da Música é a loja que fala a verdade sobre instrumentos, até quando a verdade é "esse mais barato resolve".

Formula version (for downstream agents):

For **beginner and intermediate musicians in the Centro-Oeste** who **want to get the instrument choice right without being buried in specs or pushed up the price ladder**, Empório da Música is the **instruments-only music store** that **tells you the truth about what fits your hands, your style and your budget, even when that means spending less**, because **it has been a Campo Grande store since 2008, sells nothing but instruments (no accessory upsell to hide margin in), and checks real stock, real prices and real policies before every answer.**

### Why this is ownable

- A marketplace cannot say "esse mais barato resolve": it has no person, only ranking and ads.
- A chain cannot say it credibly: its floor staff is measured on ticket size.
- Empório already has the proof in its own rules: promo prices always shown next to the original price and the %, never quoting price/stock/deadlines without the system (manual 6.2, 7.1).

Swap test: replace "Empório da Música" with Mercado Livre, Amazon or a national chain and the statement becomes false. It passes.

## White Space Analysis

**The space:** close + advises, including advising the customer to spend less. Nobody in the regional market claims it out loud.

**Validation**

| Test | Evidence |
|------|----------|
| Is there demand? | Luísa's explicit trust signal: being told "you don't need to spend more". Her distrust list (pushy upsell, fake urgency) is the chains' default behavior. |
| Can Empório credibly claim it? | 15+ years in Campo Grande; curated instruments-only catalog; manual mandates transparent promo pricing and system-checked facts. |
| Is it defensible? | Honesty compounds: every "esse mais barato resolve" becomes a referral (Luísa's discovery is friends and YouTube teachers). Marketplaces structurally cannot hold a conversation; chains structurally cannot give up the upsell. |

**What the white space demands in practice (non-negotiable behaviors)**

1. Recommend by fit (style, hands, level, budget) before recommending by price.
2. When a cheaper option fits, say it first and say why.
3. Never create urgency that the system does not confirm (no "últimas unidades" unless stock says so).
4. Promo always shows original price + % + final price.
5. If the system has no answer, say so and route to a human; never guess.

**Risk:** "honest" is a claim every brand makes. It only works if it is demonstrated, not declared. The brand never says "somos honestos"; it shows it by recommending the R$ 599,90 Yamaha C40 over the R$ 849 C70 when that is what fits.
